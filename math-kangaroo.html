How to Use

  1. Pick a grade level — Choose the level that matches your needs (or higher for a challenge)
  2. Select a year — Start with older papers and work forward, or pick your target year
  3. Practice questions — Work through the questions under time pressure for authentic practice
  4. Check answers — Review the answer key to see which questions you missed and why
  5. Track progress — Keep a record of your scores to monitor improvement over time
